Before converting, make a brief survey of the part and of the book around it: its contents, any key to its symbols, a search for each label the part uses, and one or two pages of each kind of section. Write what you find as a book map in the format below, in a message of its own, every section filled or marked unknown and each line with the pages it rests on; then go straight on to convert, with no reply needed. Find your way with the map and take everything you copy from the pages. Where a page shows the map wrong, follow the page, and say in your notes what was wrong.
