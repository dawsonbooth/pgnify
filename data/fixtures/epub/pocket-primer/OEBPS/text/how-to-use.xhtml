How to Use This Book

Chapter 2 gives moves in figurine notation: ♔ king, ♕ queen, ♖ rook, ♗ bishop and ♘ knight. A pawn move has no figurine, so ♘f3 is a knight move and e4 a pawn move.

Chapter 1 and the solutions write the pieces with English letters instead: Kg7 is a king move and Rb1 a rook move.

A game can also be given as PGN, which the book prints like this:

1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6
3. Bb5 a6

The caption under each diagram says who is to move. The printed edition showed it with the symbol instead.
